01 / The answer

What is VaultBox?

VaultBox is an iOS privacy app for hiding and encrypting personal photos, videos and documents. It is not an app hider for other installed apps, a social network, or a VaultBox-operated cloud gallery.

07 / FAQ

Private photo vault questions

Direct answers to the questions people ask before trusting a privacy app.

How does VaultBox hide photos on iPhone?

Import photos and videos into VaultBox, where they are encrypted and protected by your private PIN and Face ID. You can then remove the originals from Apple Photos when you are ready.

Does VaultBox upload my private photos to its own servers?

No. VaultBox encrypts your files on your device. If you enable encrypted backup, the backup is stored in your iCloud account rather than on a VaultBox photo-storage server.

Can VaultBox look like a calculator?

Yes. VaultBox can disguise its icon and opening screen as everyday apps including a working calculator, notes, weather or clock.

Is VaultBox an app hider?

No. VaultBox does not hide or lock other installed apps. It hides and encrypts photos, videos and documents inside its own private vault.

Is VaultBox different from the iPhone Hidden album?

Yes. VaultBox adds a separate private PIN, encrypted file storage, app disguises, a decoy vault, break-in alerts and optional encrypted backup to your iCloud account.

Is VaultBox free?

VaultBox is free to download and explore. Some advanced privacy protections require an optional in-app subscription.
